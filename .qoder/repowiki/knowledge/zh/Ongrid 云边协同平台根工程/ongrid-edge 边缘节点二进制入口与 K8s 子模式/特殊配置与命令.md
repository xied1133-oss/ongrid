二进制支持多种子命令：`--version/--help` 直接打印版本；`enroll --manager-url <HTTPS> --output <env-file>` 完成首次注册；`prepare-k8s-upgrade --namespace ... --controller ... --metrics-scraper ... --gateway-mode ... --metrics-mode ... --timeout` 用于 K8s 滚动升级前准备；`install-k8s-host-runtime <host-root> <uid> <gid>` 与 `enter-k8s-host <host-root> <uid> <gid>` 由容器侧调用以安装宿主运行环境；正常运行通过环境变量 `ONGRID_EDGE_MODE`、`ONGRID_K8S_*`、`ONGRID_ENROLLMENT_TOKEN`、`ONGRID_K8S_TELEMETRY_SECRET_DIR` 等切换行为。
